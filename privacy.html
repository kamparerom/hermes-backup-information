Privacy Policy

Hermes Backup Drive is operated privately by its account owner.

Data use

The utility accesses Google Drive only to create, read, update, and manage encrypted backup data belonging to the account owner. It does not collect data for advertising, analytics, sale, or sharing with third parties.

Security

Backup content is encrypted before remote storage. Access credentials remain only in the private operating environment required for backup execution.

Contact

For policy questions, use the support contact shown in the Google consent screen.
